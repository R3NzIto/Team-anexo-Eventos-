## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is a fighting-game player-select screen. Two players, two doors: P1 "Quiero competir" and P2 "Quiero llevar la competencia a mi espacio", equal weight, split by a diagonal, with the Anexo clip as the VS. Refuses the centered link-in-bio stack and the headline-over-cards hero.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal #1E1E1E ground with the faint yellow repeating-logo pattern; P1 side owned by yellow #FFF001 with black ink, P2 side owned by Premier purple #553589/#3B1858 with white ink. Rubik Black/Bold headlines in caps, select-screen chrome: hard diagonal cuts, player tags (P1/P2 badges), cursor brackets on focus, "READY" lock states. Real event photos as full-bleed duotone panels (yellow/black on P1, purple/white on P2), like fighter portraits behind the select cursor.

STORY: Visitor recognizes the select screen in one beat, picks a side, and the page continues as that player's route: P1 → próximo torneo, series, resultados, comunidad; P2 → qué hacemos, socios, contacto. Both routes live on the same page; both reach contact/community.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: logo left, P1/P2 jump links right. Full-height split by a ~12° diagonal: left yellow panel with P1 tag, "QUIERO COMPETIR", next event card (date, venue, price) and primary button "Inscribirme"; right purple panel with P2 tag, "LLEVÁ LA COMPETENCIA A TU ESPACIO", partner strip and button "Contactar". Center: large white clip/VS medallion on the seam. On phones the diagonal becomes horizontal: P1 on top, VS, P2 below, both buttons above the fold within two screens.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Hover/focus on a side "selects" it: the panel widens along the diagonal, its portrait brightens, the other dims, the tag flips to "READY"; click scrolls into that route. Reduced motion: instant state change, no widen.
